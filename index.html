    Principles

    How I Approach Architecture

    1. 01

      Business-first Architecture

      Start with business workflows and domain capabilities before selecting technologies.

    2. 02

      Clear Service Boundaries

      Define services around business responsibilities rather than simply splitting functionality into technical layers.

    3. 03

      Reliable Distributed Processing

      Design asynchronous workflows with retries, idempotency, failure handling and observability.

    4. 04

      Data Consistency

      Treat transactional integrity, concurrency and data correctness as first-class architectural concerns.

    5. 05

      API & Integration Contracts

      Design explicit contracts with validation, versioning, error handling and backward compatibility.

    6. 06

      Production-minded Engineering

      Architecture decisions consider deployment, monitoring, security, performance, disaster recovery and operational support from the beginning.

    7. 07

      Pragmatic Technology Choices

      Use the simplest architecture that satisfies scalability, maintainability and business requirements.
